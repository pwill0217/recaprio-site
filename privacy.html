Privacy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Your videos never leave your computer. Recaprio does all of its work on your PC: finding moments, transcribing speech, adding captions and rendering clips. There is no account, and no video, audio, script or caption is ever uploaded.

What uses the internet

  • Free export counter (Free only). Free includes 3 exports a week, counted online so the count survives reinstalling. Before each free export, Recaprio sends Recaprio's counter (hosted on Cloudflare) an anonymous code for your computer: a one-way scramble (SHA-256 hash) of your Windows machine ID, which can't be turned back into the ID or linked to your name. The counter also records a hash of your IP address, so one network can't claim unlimited exports, and it never stores the IP address itself. It keeps only weekly counts, deleted after about six weeks. Pro never uses the counter.
  • License checks (Pro only). When you activate Pro, and when Recaprio starts, it sends your license key and your computer's name to Lemon Squeezy to confirm the license is valid.
  • One-time model downloads. The first time you use a voice, captions or Pro sound analysis, Recaprio downloads that model (from Hugging Face or this website). The model then runs on your PC.
  • Update checks. Recaprio downloads a small file from this website to see whether a newer version exists. Like any website, the host may log your IP address.
  • Problem reports. Only if you choose to send one. It contains your Recaprio version, settings and recent log lines, never your videos.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and handles your payment details and receipts under its own privacy policy. Recaprio never sees your card details.
