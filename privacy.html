Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Your videos never leave your computer. Recaprio does all of its work on your PC: finding moments, transcribing speech, adding captions and rendering clips. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

What uses the internet

  • License checks (Pro only). When you activate Pro, and when Recaprio starts, it sends your license key and your computer's name to Lemon Squeezy to confirm the license is valid.
  • Update checks. Recaprio downloads a small file from this website to see whether a newer version exists. Like any website, the host may log your IP address.
  • Problem reports. Only if you choose to send one. It contains your Recaprio version, settings and recent log lines, never your videos.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and handles your payment details and receipts under its own privacy policy. Recaprio never sees your card details.
