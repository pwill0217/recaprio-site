Refunds

Last updated November 8, 2026

If Recaprio Pro isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, for any reason.

How to ask for one

Reply to your receipt email, or write to recapriosupport@gmail.com with the email address you bought with. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider.

Subscriptions

You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time from the link in your receipt. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again. Renewals can be refunded within 14 days of the renewal date.

After a refund

Your license key stops working and Recaprio switches back to the free version. Clips you already made stay yours.
