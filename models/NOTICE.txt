yamnet-v1.onnx

YAMNet sound event classifier by Google, converted to ONNX for Recaprio.
Original model and weights: https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet
Copyright 2019 The TensorFlow Authors. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Changes: the log-mel filterbank is folded into the network, which takes STFT
magnitude patches [N, 96, 257] and outputs the original 521 class scores.
Verified to match the original model's scores to within 0.0003.
